# SmartNVO Login Style Primer

## Goal

Create a warm, modern Bulgarian login experience for SmartNVO that feels educational, polished, and light.

## Core direction

- Language: Bulgarian UI copy
- Brand mark: keep the sigma symbol visible
- Tone: friendly, focused, credible
- Visual mood: soft pink and white, not neon, not glossy
- Audience: students preparing for math exams

## Color direction

- Base background: warm white or blush white
- Accent background: pale pink gradients
- Primary accent: rose or watermelon pink
- Secondary accent: muted coral or soft berry
- Text: deep charcoal, not pure black
- Support tones: dusty pink, light beige, very soft gray

## What to avoid

- No heavy shadows
- No dark-mode-first look
- No emoji-driven UI
- No loud gamer styling
- No glassmorphism overload
- No purple-heavy palette

## Layout guidance

- Balanced two-column hero/login composition on desktop
- Strong single-column stacking on mobile
- Clear sign-in card with generous spacing
- Supporting marketing/value copy should stay secondary to the form
- Sign-up CTA should be visible but not louder than sign-in

## Typography

- Use expressive but readable typography
- Heading should feel confident and slightly editorial
- Body text should stay clean and soft
- Preserve strong contrast and easy scanning

## Motion and atmosphere

- Subtle falling math symbols in the background
- Motion should be calm and ambient, never distracting
- Prefer gentle fade and drift instead of bounce or flashy transforms

## Required login UX elements

- Generic sign-in fields
- Google sign-in option
- Guest access option if supported
- Sign-up CTA leading to registration
- Clear Bulgarian helper and error text

## Interaction feel

- Inputs should feel soft and precise
- Buttons should feel tactile without large shadow stacks
- Hover states can brighten slightly or shift tone subtly
- Focus states should be obvious and accessible

## Content cues

- Speak to exam preparation, progress, and confidence
- Keep copy short and direct
- Avoid corporate or startup-style jargon

## Success criteria

- Instantly reads as math-learning product
- Feels premium without looking expensive or flashy
- Works cleanly on both desktop and mobile
- Preserves SmartNVO identity and sigma mark
