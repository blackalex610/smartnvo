# Математика — Presentation Slides
### AI-Powered Math Learning Platform for Bulgarian Students

---

## Slide 1 — Title

# Математика
### Your Personal AI Math Tutor
**An intelligent learning platform for Bulgarian 5th–7th grade students**

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem

**Students preparing for math exams face real challenges:**

- No personalized guidance — every student learns at the same pace
- Theory is hard to find in Bulgarian and student-friendly language
- NVO (national 7th-grade exam) practice is limited and generic
- Students don't know *which* topics they actually need to improve

---

## Slide 3 — What Is Математика?

**A full-stack, AI-powered web platform built for Bulgarian middle schoolers**

- Covers the complete math curriculum for grades 5, 6, and 7
- Combines structured lessons, practice exercises, and AI tutoring
- Available in the browser — no installation needed
- Built with React + TypeScript (frontend) and Python FastAPI (backend)

---

## Slide 4 — Structured Curriculum

**Every topic is organized in a clear, progressive hierarchy:**

- **Grades** (5, 6, 7) → **Topics** → **Lessons** → **Exercises**
- Students always know exactly where they are in the curriculum
- Easy navigation: pick a grade, drill down to a specific lesson
- All content is in Bulgarian and aligned to the school syllabus

---

## Slide 5 — AI-Generated Theory

**Each lesson has full theory content written by AI in Bulgarian:**

- Theory is generated on-demand by GPT for the exact lesson and grade
- Structured sections: *What you'll learn*, *Explanation*, *Examples*, *Common mistakes*, *Quick check*
- Age-appropriate language — written as a real Bulgarian teacher would explain it
- Always up-to-date — no stale textbook content

---

## Slide 6 — Lesson Videos

**Every lesson comes with curated YouTube video support:**

- Automatically searches for Bulgarian-language math videos for each concept
- Falls back to AI-generated alternative search queries if no direct results are found
- Students can watch embedded videos directly inside the platform
- Learning through multiple formats: text theory + video explanation

---

## Slide 7 — Practice Exercises

**Students can practice with real exercises and get instant feedback:**

- Each lesson includes a set of exercises to solve
- Submit an answer → instantly know if it's correct
- Wrong answer? The full step-by-step solution is shown
- Every attempt is recorded to track progress over time

---

## Slide 8 — AI Chat Assistant

**An always-available AI tutor right inside the lesson:**

- Slide-out chat sidebar accessible from any theory page
- Aware of the current lesson — gives context-specific help
- Students can ask questions in Bulgarian, just like asking a teacher
- Powered by OpenAI — answers are accurate, clear, and helpful

---

## Slide 9 — AI Diagram Generator

**Visual geometry help generated automatically from problem text:**

- Student describes a geometry problem → AI draws the diagram
- Supports: triangles, rectangles, parallel lines, coordinate planes, cubes
- Works even without internet using a smart text-analysis fallback
- Makes abstract geometry problems visual and easier to understand

---

## Slide 10 — NVO Practice Exam

**Full simulation of Bulgaria's national 7th-grade math exam (НВО):**

- 23 questions split across two modules — just like the real exam
- 90-minute countdown timer with a question navigator panel
- Questions can be marked for review and revisited before submitting
- Multiple-choice and open-answer question types supported

---

## Slide 11 — AI Exam Generation

**Every practice exam is unique, powered by AI:**

- OpenAI generates fresh, high-quality NVO-style questions from a reference pool
- Questions are shuffled and varied so students never see the same test twice
- Includes a progress bar during generation with status messages in Bulgarian
- Falls back to a local question pool if OpenAI is unavailable — no downtime

---

## Slide 12 — Scored Results & Exam History

**After submitting, students get a full performance breakdown:**

- Total score with percentage, broken down by Module 1 and Module 2
- All questions reviewed with correct answers highlighted
- Past exam attempts are saved locally with date, duration, and score
- Students can clearly see improvement over multiple practice sessions

---

## Slide 13 — Progress Dashboard

**A personal dashboard showing the student's overall learning journey:**

- Exercises completed, overall accuracy %, topics started, lessons finished
- Color-coded accuracy: green (≥80%), yellow (50–79%), red (<50%)
- Live motivational message based on actual progress
- All stats update automatically as the student works

---

## Slide 14 — Personalized Recommendations

**The platform tells students exactly what to work on next:**

- Automatically detects weak topics (accuracy below 60%)
- Suggests specific lessons within those topics for targeted review
- Shows personalized encouragement messages based on performance
- No manual tracking needed — the system does the analysis automatically

---

## Slide 15 — Math Playground & Impact

**An interactive sandbox for exploring math visually:**

- Live LaTeX math renderer — type formulas, see them rendered instantly
- Random coordinate plane problems with plotted points
- Interactive pie chart problems to practice angle calculations
- Pre-built example snippets (fractions, equations, quadratic formula, series)

---
